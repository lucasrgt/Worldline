# M29 Remote Chunk Snapshot

The fixture compiles the locally generated mapped vanilla `NibbleArray.java`
from the pinned RetroMCP workspace and compares every block, metadata, block
light, and sky light coordinate against Worldline's decoder. No mapped source
is copied into the repository.

from two unmodified official servers. The adapter requires exact zlib
completion and the legacy four-plane size before exposing an immutable neutral
snapshot. Origins, block census, and content hashes remain observational.
