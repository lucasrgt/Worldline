## Historical branch disposition

The infrastructure, WPM doctor, Rust host pool, server matrix, GUI diagnostics,
and campaign follow-up branches are predecessors of the reviewed infrastructure
consolidation by content. Later small-file and shared-helper changes supersede
their duplicate implementations. The Atlas and five frontier worker branches
are represented by their qualified integration train. Intermediate SDK/HUD
branches are superseded by published main.

M791, rejected M792 revisions, M793, and M794 production/diagnostic branches
retain their source commits and local raw evidence. They are not promoted into
the active smoke census. M795 qualifies the exercised legacy renderer lifecycle;
it does not turn a failed performance matrix into production approval. Removed
powered-creeper/daylight API copies and fixed-wait telemetry stay retired.

The maintained M792 v7 proof remains bounded and opt-in. The distinct M795 Aero
revision qualifies only its declared source-consumer boundary. Cross-GPU entity
capture and the second dated external acceptance required for TestKit 1.0 remain
separate acceptance requirements.

## Release procedure

Regenerate the 705-scenario catalogs and package the combined TestKit sources.
Requalify changed inputs through `Gate --milestone`, promote only validated
executions through the canonical pin commands, and run the complete runtime and
smoke gates. Preserve the staging history, reconcile one commit over published
main, and require the exact orchestrator authorization and CI before pushing main.
Historical worktrees and raw evidence remain preserved; duplicate uncommitted
copies are reconciled only after their integrated content has been checked.
