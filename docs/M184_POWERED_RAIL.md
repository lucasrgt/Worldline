# M184 powered rail

M184 opens the official powered-rail placement boundary. Packet15 of
powered-rail item `27` on a raised stone support places block `27:0`.
Direction occupies the low 3 bits as north-south `0`; powered bit 8 stays
0 without redstone. That exact cell remains after a clean save plus
fresh login.

This milestone does not claim detector rails, regular rails, redstone
powering, or minecart motion on powered rail.
