# M382 portal-obsidian set

M382 opens the official compound portal-obsidian set. Packet15 places
fourteen obsidian `49` cells as a complete `4x5` frame. Packet15 of
flint-and-steel item `259` then fills the six interior cells with portal
`90:0`. The frozen signal includes the multi-cell obsidian frame and the
portal interior together.

This is distinct from shipping M216 (one obsidian cell), M268 (stone
flint-and-steel fire), and from M132/M133/M134 portal travel. M382
claims frame construction plus activation only. It does not claim
Packet9, dimension change, Nether generation, or roundtrip travel.
Headless `B173WireClient` only. No GUI. No Aero.
