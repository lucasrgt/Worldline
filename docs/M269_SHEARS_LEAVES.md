# M269 shears leaves

M269 opens the official shears-versus-bare-hand oak-leaves harvest
boundary. It reuses the M209 raised stone plus adjacent oak log `17` so
placed leaves persist as `18:8`. Packet14 while holding shears item `359`
breaks one leaf to air and drops Packet21 stack `18:1:0`. Packet14 with
an empty hand breaks the second leaf to air without a new Packet21 leaf
stack.

This milestone reuses the existing Packet21 tracker. It does not claim
sapling chance, shears durability, cobweb harvest, or other leaf species.
