# M357 glowstone dust crafts

M357 opens the official personal 2x2 crafting boundary for glowstone block
`89` from four glowstone dust `348`. Beta 1.7.3 accepts that square recipe
and has no reverse uncraft of the block back into dust.

The actor starts with dust `348x4:0` in hotbar slot 36. Seven accepted
Packet102 transactions fill the 2x2 matrix and take result `89x1:0`. That
stack remains after a clean save plus fresh login. The frozen signal carries
both item IDs `348` and `89`.

This milestone does not claim M191 glowstone placement, Packet15, Nether
sessions, or glowstone-dust drops.
