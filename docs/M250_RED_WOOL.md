# M250 red wool

M250 opens the official red-wool placement boundary. Packet15 of wool
item `35` with damage `14` on a raised stone support places block `35:14`.
That exact cell remains after a clean save plus fresh login.

This milestone is distinct from M197 white wool `35:0`. It does not claim
other dye colors, sheep, or fire consumption. M152 already covers wool
burn beside netherrack fire.
