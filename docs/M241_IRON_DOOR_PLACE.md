# M241 iron door place

M241 opens the official iron `BlockDoor` placement boundary. Iron door item
330 is placed with Packet15 onto a raised stone shelf as two cells. Look yaw
selects the facing; this fixture freezes lower `71:0` and upper `71:8`, the
iron analogue of wooden door `64:0` / `64:8`. Those closed halves remain
after a clean save plus fresh login.

Frozen semantic SHA-256:
`a4f2a5f71fe45b70e843d41f32d6a4327eac0d654a488b059ca29eb2a2d261e6`.

This milestone does not claim redstone power, empty-hand toggle, M118's
powered-door consumer, wooden doors, stacking, hinge facing variants beyond
this official placement, or the Nether.
