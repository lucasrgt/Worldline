# M160 cake eat

M160 opens the official `BlockCake` boundary. Cake item 354 is placed with
Packet15 onto a raised stone shelf as block `92:0`. Empty-hand Packet15 then
eats one slice: metadata advances `0 -> 1` and Packet8 health restores
`17 -> 20` (three points). Beta 1.7.3 has no hunger bar; cake heals health.

Official cake has six bites via metadata. This milestone proves one bite. It
does not claim the remaining five bites, the sixth-bite air cell, crafting,
drops, or hunger-era food.
