# M79 cold page rebuild

Status: GO in Worldline v1.67.0.

M79 qualifies one explicitly armed cold transition over the exact warmed M78
four-page scene. After 300 retained intervals, a client-only hook calls the
public pinned-Aero `disposeModel` boundary for the exact model identity. No
server/common class acquires an Aero, Minecraft-client, or LWJGL dependency.

The event proves cache `4 -> 0 -> 4`, four deleted page lists, four newly
compiled cached pages, four page rebuilds, four page calls, sixteen enqueues,
and zero direct fallbacks. Cumulative compiled/deleted baselines may reflect
pre-bracket synchronization; the exact event deltas are both four. Every other
retained record remains on M78's warmed `rebuilds=0` path.

The disposal span is recorded in a 68-byte post-seal artifact. M78's paged
sidecar identifies the same event record and contains its renderer, enqueue,
flush, and page counters; M74 supplies the complete interval plus exact
fixture state. All three artifacts share nonce, plan, record count, and census
duration and are strictly reparsed after normal client exit.

Two fresh same-plan/nonce replicas placed the event at record 300. Disposal
spans were `58700` and `54900 ns`; event flush spans were `94100` and
`82300 ns`. Those observations are descriptive under nested instrumentation,
not release thresholds or isolated costs.
