# M324 furnace rest smelts

M324 is the remaining official furnace-smelt set after M296. One cycle
places three idle furnaces `61:2` on a raised stone fixture and smelts
sand `12` to glass `20`, cobblestone `4` to stone `1`, and raw fish `349`
to cooked fish `350`. Each recipe uses coal `263` and Packet100/102/103/105
evidence.

This milestone is distinct from M296 iron `15`, gold `14`, and pork `319`.
It does not claim pickup, experience, charcoal, clay brick, cactus green,
alternate fuels, or burning furnace `62` as a placement product. Headless
`B173WireClient` protocol-14 only. No GUI. No Aero.
