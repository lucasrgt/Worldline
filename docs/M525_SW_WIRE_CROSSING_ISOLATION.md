# M525-SW wire crossing isolation

M525-SW freezes two dust components that cross in projection but are separated
by a solid bridge. Powering the lower line leaves the upper line at zero.
Removing the lower connector isolates and depowers its tail. Powering the upper
line after removing the lower source reverses the powered/unpowered roles.

Exact IDs and metadata must match in two Worldline and two official-JAR runs.
