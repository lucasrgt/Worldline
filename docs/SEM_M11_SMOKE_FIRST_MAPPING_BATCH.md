# SEM-M11 Smoke-First Mapping Batch

SEM-M11 is the first cumulative qualification boundary for the maintained
Beta 1.7.3 symbol graph. It selects 1,619 of 6,475 independently corroborated
identities, or 25 percent rounded up. Symbols mentioned by maintained smoke
source, descriptors, and maps are ordered before untouched identities.

Qualification is about exact identity, not automatic semantic-name adoption.
An identity needs presence in at least two pinned, independently maintained
sources among Calamus, Nostalgia, Feather, and RetroMCP. Different readable
aliases remain visible and do not become Worldline names merely because the
identity is qualified.

The exact policy is `mappings/b1.7.3/sem-m11.properties`. It binds the existing
SEM-M6 official-bytecode coverage report, the enriched graph, all counts, the
ordered selected rows, the excluded rows, and the final report digest.
