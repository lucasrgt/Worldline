# M151 netherrack fire

M151 opens the official fire-material boundary. Flint and steel Packet15 on
netherrack `87:0` places fire `51:0` in the air cell above. The fire remains
after a 40-tick live hold and after a clean save plus fresh login.

This is the Beta 1.7.3 netherrack exception: the flame is not a random-tick
extinguish candidate. The milestone does not claim wood/leaf consumption,
cross-block spread, rain extinguishing, fire charge, or Nether-dimension
ignition.
