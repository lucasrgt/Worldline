# M323 iron tool crafts

M323 continues the official workbench tool-craft SET. Iron ingots `265`
plus sticks `280` in a placed workbench `58` produce iron sword `267`,
pickaxe `257`, shovel `256`, axe `258`, and hoe `292`. Packet102 result
takes carry Packet200 craft statistics and persist after save.

The frozen semantic SHA-256 is
`17587efabb76f538dcae2f11569071d321ff253c298e403c9de331483b463270`.

This milestone does not claim tool use, durability, smelting, or personal
2x2 crafting. It is distinct from M298 wood, M299 stone, and M318 gold
and diamond tool crafts.
