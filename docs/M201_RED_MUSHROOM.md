# M201 red mushroom

M201 opens the official red-mushroom placement boundary. Packet15 of red
mushroom item `40` on dirt under a dark stone pocket places block `40:0`.
That exact cell remains after a clean save plus fresh login.

Official `BlockMushroom` stays only when full light is below 13, so the
fixture is dirt under a one-block roof with opaque walls rather than a
bright stone shelf.

This milestone does not claim brown mushroom `39`, huge mushrooms, or stew.
