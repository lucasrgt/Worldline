# M400 remaining torch faces

M400 opens the official remaining wall-torch attachment set. Packet15 of
torch item `50` on the four side faces of one raised stone support places
`50:1`, `50:2`, `50:3`, and `50:4` in one session. The frozen signal names
those four damages. All four cells survive a clean save plus fresh login.

This is distinct from shipping M175 (floor torch `50:5` place-only) and
from shipping M312 (redstone-torch invert `76:4 -> 75:4`). It does not
claim floor torch `50:5`, redstone torch `75`/`76`, or the lighting plane.
Headless `B173WireClient` only. No GUI. No Aero.
