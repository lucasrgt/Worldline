# M236 cobble slab

M236 opens the official cobblestone-slab placement boundary. Packet15 of
slab item `44` with damage `3` on a raised stone support places block
`44:3`. That exact cobble-slab metadata remains after a clean save plus
fresh login.

This milestone does not re-qualify stone slab `44:0` (M188). It does not
claim sandstone or wood slab variants.
