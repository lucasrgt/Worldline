# M601 cobweb slowdown retraction

M601 was reserved as `m601-cobweb-slow-set`, but its branch and worktree never
contained a milestone implementation. The reservation is a clean husk at its
base commit, so there is no runtime evidence to preserve or qualify.

The proposed public behavior is already covered by M317. That accepted
milestone places and persists cobweb item `30` and soul sand item `88`, then
executes a four-process mapped/official client differential over air, cobweb,
and soul-sand corridors. Its frozen client-physics signature records distinct
eight-tick displacement for all three surfaces and explicitly excludes
Packet13 pose as the slowdown oracle.

Creating another cobweb-only milestone would therefore duplicate the existing
`client-runtime-equivalence` contract without adding a new observation. The
`cobweb-slow` token is formally retracted from M601 in favor of M317. The empty
reservation remains preserved until the lifecycle audit classifies it as a
husk and the coordinator archives it through the recoverable bundle route.
