item `44` with damage `1` on a raised stone support places block `44:1`.
That exact sandstone-slab metadata remains after a clean save plus fresh
login.

This milestone does not re-qualify stone slab `44:0` (M188) or double slab
`43` (M211). It does not claim wood or cobblestone slab variants.
