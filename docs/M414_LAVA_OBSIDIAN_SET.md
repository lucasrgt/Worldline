# M414 lava obsidian set

M414 opens the official compound still-lava plus water obsidian family.
Lava bucket `327` places still lava `11:0` into two raised stone basins;
water bucket `326` places still water `9:0` beside each lava source.
Official neighbor processing hardens both lava-source cells to obsidian
`49:0`. The frozen signal names lava `11`, water `9`, and two obsidian
`49` cells.

This is distinct from shipping M139 (one block-item two-cell reaction),
from shipping M344 (bucket place plus pickup with no hardening), and from
obsidian-frame portal lighting. It claims only lava-source `11` versus
cobble-producing flowing lava. Headless `B173WireClient` only. No GUI.
No Aero.
