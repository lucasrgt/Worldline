# M347 gold diamond hoes

M347 continues the official workbench tool-craft SET with gold hoe `294`
and diamond hoe `293`. Gold ingots `266` and diamonds `264` plus sticks
`280` in a placed workbench `58` produce those two hoes. Packet102 result
takes carry Packet200 craft statistics and persist after save.

The frozen semantic SHA-256 is
`db8eed6611d61cb0063b32dcf170a0fd66d46799102196c45721842544e6515b`.

This milestone does not claim tool use, durability, farmland tilling, or
personal 2x2 crafting. It is distinct from M318 gold and diamond
sword/pick/axe/shovel crafts and from M323 iron hoe `292`.
