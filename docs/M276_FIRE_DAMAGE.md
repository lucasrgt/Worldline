# M276 fire damage

M276 opens the official fire-damage boundary. Flint and steel Packet15 on
netherrack `87:0` places fire `51:0` in the air cell above. Standing in that
netherrack flame drops Packet8 health `20 -> 19` by one point. The actor then
steps off the flame so the saved health remains 19 after a fresh login.

This milestone does not claim flame persistence (M151), wool consumption
(M152), lava damage, armor reduction, burning duration, or death.
