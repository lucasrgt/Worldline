# M281 light blue wool

M281 opens the official light-blue-wool placement boundary. Packet15 of
wool item `35` with damage `3` on a raised stone support places block
`35:3`. That exact cell remains after a clean save plus fresh login.

This milestone is distinct from other wool metas, including M197 white
wool `35:0`. It does not claim other dye colors, sheep, or fire
consumption.
