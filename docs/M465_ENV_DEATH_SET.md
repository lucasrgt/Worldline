# M465 environmental death set

M465 opens the official environmental-death SET. Seeded health 20 is written
before each of three official logins. Two-deep still water `8/9` over the head
drowns Packet8 to 0. Falling sand `12` on the head cell suffocates Packet8 to
0. Still lava `11:0` from lava bucket `327` then burns Packet8 to 0. Packet38
status 2 precedes those health packets. One cycle hashes all three deaths.

This family is distinct from M307 drowning, suffocation, and lava hurt
`20 -> 19` without death, from M461 fall, and from M469 void. It does not
claim cactus, fire standing-damage, armor reduction, or Packet9 respawn.
