# M418 remaining obsidian place

M418 opens the official remaining obsidian frame-place family. Packet15
places four obsidian `49` cells as an L-shaped portal-frame fragment on
the raised stone fixture. The interior cell stays air. This session does
not hold flint-and-steel `259` and does not light portal `90`.

Packet14 while holding diamond pickaxe `278` then breaks the cap cell of
that fragment to air and drops Packet21 obsidian `49:1:0`. The other three
`49` cells stay placed and unlit. The frozen signal names multiple `49`
cells and the pick harvest of `49`.

This is distinct from shipping M216 (one obsidian Packet15), from M375
(one obsidian among mixed remaining hard blocks), and from sibling M382
(complete fourteen-cell frame plus flint-and-steel portal `90`). It does
not claim portal activation, Packet9, dimension change, or M139
water-lava hardening. Headless `B173WireClient` only. No GUI. No Aero.
