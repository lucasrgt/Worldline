# M162 wooden door

M162 opens the official wooden `BlockDoor` boundary. Wooden door item 324 is
placed with Packet15 onto a raised stone shelf as two cells: lower `64:0` and
upper `64:8`. Empty-hand Packet15 on the lower half opens the door to lower
`64:4` / upper `64:12`, then a second activation closes it back to the placed
metadata. The closed door remains after a clean save plus fresh login.

Frozen semantic SHA-256:
`d14b3dc599ec9ecd4d0f39074ae46e401c37bd078d558f4b0dd0b477a3f1bfea`.

This milestone does not claim iron doors, redstone power, stacking, hinge
facing variants beyond this official placement, or the Nether.
