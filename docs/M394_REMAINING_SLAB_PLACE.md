sandstone slab `44:1`, wood slab `44:2`, and cobble slab `44:3` on
adjacent raised stone pads in one session. Double-slab item `43` is
natural in this family and places `43:0` on the south pad. The frozen

from shipping M336 (workbench crafts of `44:1`/`44:2`/`44:3`). It does
not re-qualify the 1:1 place traces M234, M235, M236, or M211. Headless
`B173WireClient` only. No GUI. No Aero.
