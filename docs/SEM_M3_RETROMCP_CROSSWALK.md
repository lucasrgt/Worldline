members only through exact official owner, name, and JVM descriptor identity;
textual similarity is never a join key.

## Non-claims

The crosswalk proves reproducible namespace resolution, not behavioral
meaning. It does not claim that RetroMCP or Nostalgia names are correct, that
unmatched methods are absent from official bytecode, or that a side-specific
name disagreement is an error. Official-JAR evidence remains necessary for
semantic promotion.
